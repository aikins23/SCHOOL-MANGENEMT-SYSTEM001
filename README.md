# Nyansapo School ERP 🇬🇭

Welcome to **Nyansapo School ERP**! This comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning solution is designed to untangle the complexities of school management in the Ghanaian educational landscape, offering a range of professional features tailored to meet the diverse needs of modern educational institutions.

## Features

### Student Admission/Registration
Effortlessly enroll new students into your school with our intuitive admission and registration module. Capture essential information, including personal details, academic history, and contact information, to maintain accurate student records.

### Staff & Employee Management
Register and manage your school's faculty and staff members efficiently. Keep track of employee details, qualifications, and roles within the institution to ensure smooth operations and effective communication.

### Financial Ledger & Fees
A robust, term-specific accounting system to track fee collections, outstanding balances, and generate professional receipts.

### Exams & Academic Reporting
Automate the grading process and generate high-quality terminal reports and analytics dashboards for school directors.

### Library & Resource Management
Catalog books, track loans, and manage school resources with integrated fine calculations.
