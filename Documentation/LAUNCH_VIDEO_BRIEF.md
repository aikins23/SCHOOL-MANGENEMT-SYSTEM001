# AI Video Production Brief: Nyansapo School ERP Launch

## 1. Project Goal
To create a 60-90 second professional launch video for **Nyansapo School ERP**. The video should introduce the brand name, explain the cultural significance of the name, showcase the software's modern interface, and demonstrate how it solves administrative challenges for Ghanaian schools.

## 2. Visual & Audio Style
*   **Tone:** Professional, high-tech, trustworthy, and inspirational.
*   **Color Palette:** Deep Navy (#0B1F49) and Metallic Gold (#C59E39).
*   **Visual Style:** A mix of clean high-resolution screen recordings of the software, minimalist animations of the Nyansapo knot, and cinematic stock footage of vibrant Ghanaian school environments.
*   **Music:** A modern, upbeat corporate track with subtle West African rhythmic elements (e.g., kpanlogo or highlife-inspired percussion) that feels "tech-forward."
*   **Voiceover:** A professional, warm, and authoritative West African (Ghanaian) male or female voice.

---

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Script (Storyboard)

| Scene | Visual Instruction | Audio / Voiceover Script |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00:00 - 00:10** | **The Problem:** Close-up of messy, piled-up paper student files and a frustrated administrator at a desk. Fast-paced editing. | "Managing a school in the modern era shouldn't feel like a struggle with the past. Paper records and disconnected data are holding you back." |
| **00:10 - 00:20** | **The Reveal:** Cinematic transition to a gold Nyansapo knot slowly rotating and "untangling" itself on a navy background. | "Introducing Nyansapo School ERP. Inspired by the Akan symbol of wisdom, we've untangled the complexities of school administration." |

## 4. Specific AI Tool Prompts

### For Logo Animation (Motion Graphics):
> "Animate a minimalist gold Nyansapo knot symbol on a deep navy blue background. The knot should smoothly draw itself using continuous lines, glowing slightly as it completes. The text 'Nyansapo School ERP' should fade in elegantly beneath it. High-end SaaS software intro style."

### For Cinematic Stock Footage:
> "Cinematic 4k footage of a modern, well-lit classroom in Accra, Ghana. A teacher is using a laptop while smiling students work in the background. Soft morning light, professional atmosphere, depth of field."

### For Voiceover Generation:
> "Generate a professional, calm, and inspiring voiceover with a clear Ghanaian accent. The tone should be authoritative like a tech CEO but warm and welcoming. Pacing should be moderate with pauses for emphasis on key words like 'Wisdom' and 'Untangled'."

## 5. Technical Requirements
*   **Resolution:** 1080p or 4K.
*   **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Main Launch) and 9:16 (Shorts/TikTok/WhatsApp Status).
*   **Output Format:** MP4.
